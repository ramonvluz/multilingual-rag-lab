Summary

DOC-021#SEC-01

On 2026-05-07, outbound webhook delivery in US-EAST was delayed for 47 minutes. No accepted events were lost. Approximately 18% of affected deliveries arrived more than fifteen minutes late. The incident was classified P1 because multiple Enterprise workspaces could not complete revenue workflows.

Timeline

DOC-021#SEC-02

At 14:02 UTC, provider latency increased. At 14:08, RL-4290 rose and the generic retry policy added load. At 14:16, queue age crossed fifteen minutes. At 14:24, the team reduced connector concurrency. At 14:39, the provider recovered. At 14:49, queue age returned below five minutes.

Root cause

DOC-021#SEC-03

A connector adapter ignored Retry-After values above 120 seconds and substituted its 30-second default. Requests reached the destination before its rate bucket recovered, amplifying throttling. The queue and storage layers operated normally. The proximate trigger was external latency; the customer impact was amplified by OrbeFlow retry behavior.

Corrective actions

DOC-021#SEC-04

Adapter version 3.4.2 now accepts Retry-After up to 900 seconds. Monitoring separates throttling from generic failure. The event delivery runbook now requires destination-specific throttling before replay. Action ACT-771 adds a regression test for long Retry-After values.
